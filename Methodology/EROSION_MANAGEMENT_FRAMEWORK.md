# Economic Resilience: Volusia County Erosion Management Framework

## Definition: Economic Erosion
Economic erosion refers to the gradual decline or weakening of an economic sector or community's economic position, often imperceptible until significant damage has occurred.

## Erosion Patterns in Volusia

### Vulnerable Sectors
1. **Retail** - Competition from e-commerce, changing consumer preferences
2. **Hospitality** - Seasonal dependence, climate vulnerability  
3. **Manufacturing** - Offshoring pressure, automation displacement
4. **Logistics** - Market saturation, rate pressure

### Protective Sectors
1. **Healthcare** - Demographics-driven demand, essential services
2. **Education** - Public funding stability, mission-critical
3. **Government** - Tax base dependency but stable funding
4. **Aviation** - Specialized expertise, regional uniqueness

## Erosion Detection Metrics

### Leading Indicators
- Median wage stagnation (sector wage < inflation)
- Job posting decline (>20% YoY)
- Employee count reduction over 12 months
- Website/content update frequency
- Social media engagement decline

### Lagging Indicators
- Business closure announcements
- Workforce data gaps in jobs reports
- Reduced tax revenue attribution
- Permit application declines

## Volusia-Specific Erosion Risks

### Coastal Tourism Dependency
- **Risk**: Climate impacts, hurricane damage
- **Mitigation**: Diversify tourism offerings, invest in resilient infrastructure

### Seasonal Employment Concentration
- **Risk**: Economic volatility, labor market instability  
- **Mitigation**: Year-round employers (healthcare, education), skills transfer programs

### Single-Employer Concentration
- **Risk**: Economic shock if major employer departs
- **Mitigation**: Support SME development, foster entrepreneurship

## Erosion Intervention Framework

### Tier 1: Early Detection
- Automated job board monitoring
- News sentiment analysis for sector keywords
- Website change detection for major employers

### Tier 2: Pattern Analysis
- Cross-sector correlation mapping
- Workforce mobility tracking
- Supply chain vulnerability assessment

### Tier 3: Intervention Design
- Stakeholder coalition building
- Resource mapping and gap analysis
- Proposal development with timelines

## Erosion Response Playbook

### When a Major Employer Shows Signs
1. **Assessment** - Verify data through 3+ sources
2. **Notification** - Contact workforce partners, economic development
3. **Analysis** - Map skills, supply chain, ripple effects
4. **Response** - Coordinate retention/transition strategies

### When a Sector Shows Systemic Signs
1. **Sector Audit** - Inventory all employers in sector
2. **Community Impact** - Estimate job losses, tax impact
3. **Attraction Plan** - Identify replacement interests
4. **Transition Support** - Workforce retraining, small biz support
