# Sector Classification Scheme: Project Volusia

## Classification Methodology
Employer classification follows the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) with local adaptation for Volusia County specifics.

## Sector Definitions

### 1. Healthcare & Life Sciences
**NAICS Codes**: 62 (Health Care and Social Assistance)
**Description**: Hospitals, clinics, medical device manufacturers, pharmaceuticals, health insurance, assisted living facilities, home health care.
**Key Indicators**: Patient care, medical services, health insurance, biotechnology, medical devices.

### 2. Education
**NAICS Codes**: 61 (Educational Services)
**Description**: K-12 school districts, community colleges, universities, vocational schools, tutoring, educational technology.
**Key Indicators**: Schools, universities, student enrollment, research, teaching, curriculum development.

### 3. Government & Public Safety
**NAICS Codes**: 92 (Public Administration)
**Description**: County, city, state, and federal government agencies, law enforcement, fire departments, public utilities, transportation authorities.
**Key Indicators**: Public services, law enforcement, utilities, transportation, municipal services.

### 4. Aviation / Aerospace
**NAICS Codes**: 48 (Transportation), 33 (Manufacturing)
**Description**: Airlines, airports, flight training schools, aerospace manufacturing, aircraft parts, unmanned systems, space technology.
**Key Indicators**: Flight operations, aircraft maintenance, aerospace engineering, flight training, airport services.

### 5. Insurance & Financial Services
**NAICS Codes**: 52 (Financial and Insurance Activities)
**Description**: Insurance companies, brokerage firms, banks, credit unions, investment firms, financial planning, asset management.
**Key Indicators**: Risk management, underwriting, financial products, investment services.

### 6. Manufacturing
**NAICS Codes**: 31-33 (Manufacturing)
**Description**: Goods production including food processing, automotive parts, electronics, marine vessels, furniture, printed materials.
**Key Indicators**: Production facilities, supply chains, industrial equipment, quality control.

### 7. Logistics & Distribution
**NAICS Codes**: 49 (Transportation and Warehousing), 51 (Information)
**Description**: Shipping, freight forwarding, warehousing, fulfillment centers, package delivery, supply chain management.
**Key Indicators**: Distribution centers, shipping volumes, delivery networks, inventory management.

### 8. Retail
**NAICS Codes**: 44-45 (Retail Trade)
**Description**: Grocery stores, big box retailers, restaurants, specialty stores, e-commerce fulfillment, home improvement.
**Key Indicators**: Consumer goods, point-of-sale, customer traffic, seasonal patterns.

### 9. Hospitality & Entertainment
**NAICS Codes**: 72 (Accommodation and Food Service), 71 (Arts and Entertainment)
**Description**: Hotels, restaurants, bars, entertainment venues, sports facilities, convention centers, motorsports.
**Key Indicators**: Events, bookings, seasonal operations, tourism.

### 10. Utilities & Infrastructure
**NAICS Codes**: 22 (Utilities), 23 (Construction)
**Description**: Electric, water, gas utilities; construction firms; renewable energy; infrastructure development.
**Key Indicators**: Power generation, infrastructure projects, utility maintenance, renewable energy.

### 11. Construction
**NAICS Codes**: 23 (Construction)
**Description**: Residential builders, commercial contractors, specialty trade contractors, civil engineering.
**Key Indicators**: Building permits, project pipelines, workforce demand, material supply.

### 12. Professional Services
**NAICS Codes**: 54 (Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services)
**Description**: Consulting firms, legal services, accounting, engineering, marketing agencies, IT services.
**Key Indicators**: Expertise-based services, business support, technical consulting.

## Classification Rules

### Primary Rule
Classify by where the employer's **primary economic activity** occurs.

### Secondary Rule
When primary activity is ambiguous, use:
1. Largest employer count
2. Highest revenue generation
3. Most visible operations

### Split Classification
For employers spanning multiple sectors:
- Assign to primary sector
- Document secondary sectors in notes
- Track multi-sector jobs separately

## Classification Verification

### Data Sources for Classification
1. Employer website (about, services pages)
2. LinkedIn Company Page
3. SEC filings (for public companies)
4. Industry directories
5. Press releases and news articles

### Confidence Scoring
- **High**: 3+ consistent sources
- **Medium**: 2 sources, some ambiguity
- **Low**: 1 source, potential misclassification
