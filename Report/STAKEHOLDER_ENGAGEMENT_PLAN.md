# Stakeholder Engagement Plan: Project Volusia

## Purpose
This plan outlines engagement strategies for key stakeholder groups in Project Volusia.

## Stakeholder Categories

### 1. Government Entities
- **Volusia County Government** - Economic Development, HR
- **City of Daytona Beach** - Development, Planning
- **City of DeLand** - Economic Revitalization
- **Volusia County School Board** - Workforce development
- **State of Florida** - Jobs, Education, Economic Development

**Engagement Strategy**:
- Monthly briefings on dataset updates
- Quarterly joint analysis sessions
- Policy-relevant insights sharing
- Formal data sharing agreements (as needed)

### 2. Major Employers
- **Healthcare**: AdventHealth, Halifax Health
- **Education**: Embry-Riddle, Daytona State, VCS
- **Insurance**: Brown & Brown, Foundation Risk
- **Aviation**: Daytona Beach International
- **Logistics**: Amazon, FedEx, World Class Distribution

**Engagement Strategy**:
- Employer profile verification requests
- Sector council participation invitations
- Workforce development collaboration
- Annual sector analysis sharing

### 3. Workforce Development
- **Volusia County CareerSource** - One-stop center
- **Daytona State College** - Career services
- **Embry-Riddle** - Career placement office
- **Community agencies** - Job training programs

**Engagement Strategy**:
- Job posting integration
- Skills gap analysis sharing
- Training program coordination
- Success metrics tracking

### 4. Community Organizations
- **Economic Development Council** - Regional development
- **Chamber of Commerce** - Business advocacy
- **Nonprofit sector** - Social services, foundations
- **Labor unions** - Worker representation

**Engagement Strategy**:
- Data accessibility workshops
- Community input sessions
- Collaborative analysis projects
- Resource sharing

---

## Communication Channels

### Primary
- **Email**: direct contact with key representatives
- **GitHub Issues**: Public feedback and suggestions
- **Meetings**: In-person or virtual sector sessions

### Secondary
- **Slack/Discord**: Real-time collaboration
- **Newsletter**: Monthly project updates
- **Reports**: Quarterly public reports

## Feedback Mechanism

### Issue Reporting
1. GitHub issue template
2. Direct email to project lead
3. Stakeholder meeting discussion

### Prioritization
- **Critical**: Data accuracy affecting decisions
- **High**: Missing sectors or employers
- **Medium**: Analysis gaps or recommendations
- **Low**: Enhancement suggestions
