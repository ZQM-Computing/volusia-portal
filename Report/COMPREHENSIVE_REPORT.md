# Project Volusia: Comprehensive Economic Landscape Report

## Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Volusia County's economic landscape as of Q4 2026, synthesizing data from 72 verified employers and 29 current job opportunities. Key findings identify healthcare and manufacturing as dominant economic drivers, with retail showing vulnerability to economic erosion patterns.

## 1. Economic Overview

### 1.1 County Profile

- **Major Cities**: Daytona Beach (72K), DeLand (90K), Port Orange (65K)
- **Area**: 1,061 square miles
- **Coastline**: 18.4 miles Atlantic, 34 miles Indian River Lagoon

### 1.2 Economic Base
Volusia County's economy is diverse yet anchored by:
- **Healthcare & Life Sciences**: 12 major employers, 5 jobs available
- **Manufacturing**: 22 employers (largest employment base), 2 jobs available
- **Government**: 5 major employers, 4 jobs available

### 1.3 Employment Statistics (Estimates)
