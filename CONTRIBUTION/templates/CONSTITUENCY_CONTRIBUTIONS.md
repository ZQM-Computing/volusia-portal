# YOUTH & STUDENT CONTRIBUTION

Pathway J — Youth & Student Contributor

Young people (ages 13-25) contribute observations, ideas, digital literacy, and community energy that help Project Volusia serve its youngest residents and future leaders.

## Submission Fields

| Field | Description | Type |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Age Group** | 13-17, 18-21, 22-25 | Select |
| **School/Org** | High school, college, club, organization | Free text |
| **Contribution Type** | Observation, idea, digital project, event | Select |
| **Description** | What you're contributing | Free text |
| **Location** | Where in Volusia County? | Free text |
| **Category** | Education, jobs, mental health, environment, tech, arts | Select |
| **Urgency** | How important is this? | High/Medium/Low |
| **Permission** | Can we publish with your name? | Yes / Anonymous / Name only |

## Contribution Types

- **Digital literacy observations** — What tech resources are missing in your school/community?
- **Mental health and wellness** — What support systems exist? What's missing?
- **Education gaps** — Course availability, career counseling, internship access
- **Youth employment** — Part-time jobs, internships, apprenticeships in the area
- **Environmental stewardship** — Beach cleanups, conservation projects, sustainability ideas
- **Arts and culture** — Local events, creative projects, cultural heritage
- **Technology ideas** — App ideas, website features, digital solutions for the county
- **Community service** — Volunteering, peer mentoring, tutoring

## Guidelines

- All youth contributions are valued regardless of formal credentials
- Parental/guardian consent required for contributors under 18
- Youth data is never sold or used for advertising
- The "First Spark" mission is designed for first-time contributors
- Youth contributors earn "Explorer Visit" and "Community Voice" missions
- School group projects can submit via the School Project pathway simultaneously
